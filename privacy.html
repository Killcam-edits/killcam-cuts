Killcam Cuts — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Killcam Cuts ("the App") is a private, single-operator tool that posts the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's official API.

Data the App accesses. With the owner's authorisation, the App receives from TikTok: an access token and refresh token, the account's open ID and display name (user.info.basic), and the status and public statistics of videos the App itself uploaded (video.upload, video.publish).

Data the App does not access. No other users' data, no followers' data, no private messages, no contacts, no device data.

Storage. Tokens and post records are stored locally on the owner's own computer in a private folder. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's API.

Sharing. No data is shared with, sold to or processed by any third party.

Retention and deletion. Tokens are kept until the owner revokes the App in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) or deletes the local folder; both remove all stored data immediately.

Children. The App has no users other than its adult owner.

Changes. This policy may be updated at any time; the current version is always at this address.

Contact: the App owner, via the TikTok account the App is connected to.
